# Direct peer send assurance preparation

Status: prospective author packet; not committed, independently audited, registered or executed.\
Authority: the director selected preparation of fresh-reader questions, an answer key and frozen reading sets with "continue".\
This step authorizes no commit, independent-agent invocation, scoring run, runtime change, ratification or publication.

The question is whether the adoption guidance lets an AGP maintainer plan the right change and acceptance work without filling gaps from memory.\
Compare the reviewed contract alone with the same contract plus the adoption guidance.\
The proposed protocol below must pass key and execution-readiness review before it is used; its criteria are author judgments, not measured reader performance.

## 4. Proposed measurement and scoring

Use three fresh readers per reading, one from each of three different model families, with the same three profiles/settings on both sides: six reader cells and 72 question outcomes.\
This is a small descriptive comparison, not within-family replication or a calibrated estimate of general model performance.\
Family/model/provider identities, execution order, runtime versions and budgets must be fixed and reviewed before execution; none is selected here.

The author, the earlier design reviewer, the key auditor and sessions carrying prior AGP context are ineligible as scored readers.\
The final gate reviewer is neither author nor a scored reader.\
Judging must follow the [context-lab component contract](../../../../mission-kit/components/C2-context-lab.md): each reader's answers are judged by the other two families, never its own.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | All asked decisions are correct, sufficiently actionable and grounded; a genuinely unselected choice is identified without inventing it |
| 1 | Safe but incomplete, uncertain or insufficiently grounded; a faithful gap in needed implementation information is distinct from a wrong answer |
| 0 | Materially wrong required decision, invented authority/guarantee, or no answer |

An intended exclusion or unselected API/formula is a fully correct scoped answer.\
A document that leaves an asked implementation location or current-accounting diagnosis unresolved may produce a faithful gap but incomplete actionability; record both facts rather than call the reader wrong.\
No unasked subsidiary rule, exact wording or fixed quotation count is required.

Code checks whether each submitted excerpt occurs in the exact assigned input, normalizing whitespace only.\
Judges separately determine whether the cited premises entail the claimed decision; a matching string does not prove support.\
A missing or invalid excerpt for a positive claim caps an otherwise safe answer at 1, never raises a 0; a justified absence claim need not manufacture a quotation of absence.

Retain each judge's score and reason, then use the lower valid score and the quotation cap for the conservative score.\
Keep wrong decision, faithful information gap, missing decision, unsupported inference and quote/format defect as separate diagnostic categories.\
If a later authorized process uses the author as scorer, ties must still favor the baseline; this packet proposes independent judging, not that substitution.

## 5. Validity and decision rule

Record every expected reader/question outcome and all 144 expected other-family vote slots, including explicit not-cast entries.\
Do not promote arbitrary retained stdout, event traces or internal reasoning to reader answers: the chosen runtime contract must identify the user-visible response eligible for judging.

| Event | Prospective treatment |
|---|---|
| Completed reader has no substantive answer for a question under the pre-audited extraction contract | Zero for that missing answer, retaining raw output and an explicit reason; no invented votes; a quote/format defect in a present answer is not automatically absence |
| Present, eligible answer | Requires both valid other-family judgments; absence of required judgment leaves no comparison verdict |
| Delivery, isolation, invocation or eligibility cannot be established | No comparison verdict; retain every attempt and diagnose the gap |
| Runtime or infrastructure failure | No automatic retry is authorized; retain evidence and determine eligibility under the pre-audited runtime contract, never a favorable post-hoc reclassification |
| Wrong answer, safe gap, quote defect or control regression | Apply the prospective rule; no selective rerun, key change or reweighting |
| Substantive key, question, projection or scoring defect discovered after answers exist | No comparison verdict for the affected instrument; preserve outcomes and correct prospectively |

Before running, the selected public execution surface must demonstrate how its outcome and failure classes implement this table.\
If that cannot be established, report the compatibility gap; do not execute and decide the accounting afterwards.

A proposed documentation-assurance PASS requires all of the following:

1. All six reader cells are accounted for under validated delivery/isolation/eligibility rules; all present eligible answers have both required judgments.
2. Each candidate question has at least two conservative scores of 2 and no score of 0.
3. Each control's candidate sum is at least the baseline sum; gains elsewhere cannot compensate for a control regression.
4. At least one question has a strictly higher candidate sum; an all-tie comparison retains the baseline and supplies no demonstrated improvement.
5. Raw observations and all reasons have been inspected, with no unresolved material candidate/comparison ambiguity or measurement defect.

A valid measurement missing a condition is FAIL under this proposed rule; invalid required evidence means NO VERDICT.\
A FAIL to demonstrate improvement does not invalidate the prior technical review or reinstate a superseded identity design.

Two of three readers hedging on the same required decision triggers an ambiguity review against the supplied text before blaming the key.\
An explicitly deferred choice is not a hedge, and a baseline-only limitation is not a candidate defect.\
No result ratifies the contract, verifies runtime behavior or closes B51, B53, B6, browser or scale acceptance.

| Boundary | Required before execution |
|---|---|

| Response bound | Same 24 KiB UTF-8 response budget for every reader, with supported enforcement and explicit truncation accounting |
| Judging | Anonymous reader/question units and necessary support, no arm/order metadata; exact-quote checks and both other-family judgments retained |

### Mechanics rationale and consequence

Mechanics: freeze the questions/key and both reading sets before independent audit, with deterministic projection and integrity checks.\
Rationale: a grounded decision must come from supplied guidance rather than a warm reader's memory.\
Consequence: without controls and explicit invalid-evidence handling, a detailed packet or a failed invocation could be counted as assurance it never supplied.
