## 3. Blocking findings

### F1 — Medium: Q07 introduces an unsupported consumer obligation

The [key at line 169](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/direct-peer-answer-key.json:169) requires:

> “Validate origin and configured mode”

But the [question](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/direct-peer-questions.json:30) asks where operation-call obtains its restriction and how branches preserve it.

The reading assigns the origin-equality condition to AGP's direct-context construction:

> “populate `directPeer` only when the exact ingress is admitted, its remote node equals `source.originNodeId`”

That is [baseline line 241](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/readings/m73/direct-peer-reading.txt:241).
The expressly assigned operation-call validation is:

> “validate its configured mode against the captured association”

That is [baseline line 319](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/readings/m73/direct-peer-reading.txt:319).

Using AGP's captured origin and performing another consumer-side origin validation are different obligations.
Neither reading specifies the latter.
The key's supporting mode-validation quotation does not entail it.

Consequently, a correct answer that trusts the supplied AGP context and preserves its target can be marked incomplete for omitting a new validation step.
That makes this nominal control depend on an obligation unavailable from its baseline.

**Required correction:** require use of the captured origin and validation of configured mode, without separately scoring consumer origin validation.
If another check is genuinely intended, its ownership and the corresponding question must first be established prospectively; it cannot be supplied by the key.

### F2 — Medium: Q11's conditional request conflicts with its mandatory score cap

The [question at line 46](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/direct-peer-questions.json:46) asks for:

> “any known current accounting limitation”

The [required decision at line 279](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/direct-peer-answer-key.json:279) similarly says:

> “Identify the stated current handler-ledger limitation where available”

The baseline establishes the required accounting property:

> “account for the added metadata … rather than count only payload bytes”

See [baseline line 284](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/readings/m73/direct-peer-reading.txt:284).
It does not establish the current ledger implementation.
The specific current-state observation appears in the [candidate at line 492](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/direct-peer-send-assurance/readings/r28/direct-peer-reading.txt:492).

A reader can therefore satisfy the conditional clause by identifying that no current-ledger observation is supplied, while still specifying the required inventory, charging and release work.
The support note nevertheless mandates a downgrade on that ground alone.

This is not an objection to scoring genuinely needed missing implementation information as incomplete.
It is a mismatch between the conditional task actually asked and the mandatory diagnostic task graded.

**Required correction:** either make the current-state diagnosis an explicit required task in the common question and align the key, or retain the conditional wording and remove the automatic cap for that clause.
Continue distinguishing missing information from wrong claims, and do not infer today's implementation from a normative requirement.
