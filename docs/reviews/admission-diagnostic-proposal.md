## Frozen material and acceptance

### Judge distinctions

Reuse the seven present [literal scoring cases](admission-evaluation-preparation/admission-scoring-cases.json) and their independent semantic review, with the unchanged [policy](admission-evaluation-preparation/admission-scoring-policy.mjs).\
The exact rendered calibration packet and case-to-key mapping still require an independent pre-call audit, including the per-case contradictory source for the mixed wrong-plus-conflict case.\
Expected score order is `2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0, 0`; an absent answer produces no model call or invented raw score.

One synthetic origin label per family gives 21 present units and three absent units.\
Each judge sees 14 present units; one scenario and one answer phase with six outcomes per prompt gives three jobs per judge, nine jobs total, 42 cast-vote slots and six explicit uncast slots.\
Family labels are assignment fixtures, not claims that those models authored the literal answers; repetitions are not independent semantic observations.\
Keep all cases under one scenario with per-property keys/questions so grouping cannot silently inflate the job budget; prove the exact schedule in the capture-only pass.

Evaluate original raw judgments before conservative aggregation and quote caps.\
Every present raw score must match its fixed expectation, and an independent reviewer must check every favorable and unfavorable reason against the supplied question, key, answer and source.\
Clean reason tags alone never establish semantic correctness.\
Built-in completion retries may fill syntactically missing votes within the three-attempt job allowance, with every prompt/reply retained; an accepted unfavorable or semantically defective vote is never retried, rewritten or replaced.\
Any tool use, provider error, stop-limit event or missing raw record latches the runtime guard closed before further paid work.\
Any substantive semantic defect fails stage 2 and prevents stage 3.

### Representative reader completion

Prepare two unrelated library-policy documents with 24 distinct decision questions and an independently audited key, preserving the original one-phase answer-file and quotation duties.\
Target the measured workload envelope: instruction documents of 48,000-63,000 UTF-8 bytes, question task of 8,000-10,000 bytes, and a source-grounded reference answer of 28,000-32,000 bytes.\
Use coherent material and distinct decisions, not repeated padding; record tokenizer counts where supported and state that byte similarity alone does not establish semantic equivalence.\
Freeze the exact files before calls; the reference answer and key are withheld from readers.

Use public `runEpisode` with the real public classifier; never the injected-valid fixture used for static qualification.\
All six readers must produce the 24 answer sections, correct required decisions and decisive exact excerpts, with the answer content equal in workspace, snapshot and ledger.\
Evaluate actual semantic completeness, not an output-size minimum that would penalize a concise correct answer.\
Retain full outcomes and separately visible/reasoning tokens and finish reasons where the runtime exposes them; unknown remains unknown.\
An ended phase or absent file is a diagnostic failure, with its original disposition retained; no continuation, repair prompt, second reader attempt or model removal is allowed.\
This stage is not a two-arm AGP superiority experiment and produces no AGP adoption score.

## Stop conditions and completion

Stop before further paid work on any changed pin/setting/input, quota or deadline exhaustion, route mismatch, unobserved request, record loss, unexpected tool use, raw-score contradiction, semantic failure or incomplete reader output.\
With serial execution, independent judgment between stages prevents the next stage from starting on an unresolved prior result.\
Unexpected activity is retained and assessed, never deliberately provoked by a fault or vulnerability input.\
After an ambiguous request failure, retain its worst-case reservation; do not spend its allocation again.

The author retains complete records and performs deterministic reconciliation; a non-author reviewer decides the bounded diagnostic result.\
A resource-limited or missing-evidence run supplies no qualification verdict; a valid observation that contradicts a frozen acceptance condition is a failure.\
Neither outcome permits tuning and rerunning in the same authorization.\
Any changed cap, case, policy, model or retry rule needs a new prospective decision with the failed records intact.

Success permits proposing fresh AGP assurance preparation, not regrading old results or implementing B53.\
Real delivery/isolation requirements and whole-conductor compatibility remain distinct prerequisites for the later full comparison; this component-level diagnostic does not discharge them.\
Fresh questions/key audit, fresh assurance sessions, completed-measurement review, public-contract ratification and implementation verification remain separate.

## Rationale and alternatives

Mechanics: stage the specific judging and reader checks behind enforced resources and complete evidence, with no evaluator fork or hidden call path.\
Rationale: the earlier result cannot support adoption, and another full comparison would spend against two unresolved measurement concerns.\
Consequence: one diagnostic either supplies bounded evidence or names the exact missing capability; failure does not become a reason to rewrite AGP's proposal.

- Recommended: prepare zero-call readiness for this packet; spend remains zero until exact-profile review and director approval, with a proposed later USD 20 ceiling.
- Request an upstream budget/trace capability first if the provider-boundary requirements cannot be met through existing supported mechanisms; retain B53 as open and do not build a consumer-local substitute.
- Park this assurance path and select an independent Zorg browser/permissions design task; avoid diagnostic cost while leaving B53 adoption unresolved.
- Change the assurance gate only through an explicit director decision about sufficient evidence, not by relabeling this static or diagnostic work as assurance.

The original FAIL and later NO VERDICT stay frozen.\
This proposal grants no B56 acceptance, AGP runtime change, shared configuration mutation, push or Zorg/upstream communication.
