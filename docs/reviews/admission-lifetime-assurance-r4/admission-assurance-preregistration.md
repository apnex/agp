## Required answers and diagnostic separation

Readers answer every heading with Decision, Document support and Inference or gap, then record additional observations separately.\
Each decisive excerpt is a `QUOTE:` JSON string containing contiguous supplied-design text; normalize whitespace only when checking it, not punctuation or Markdown markers.\
The instruction makes source copying explicit, but there is no quotation count or preferred wording to satisfy.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Every required decision is correct, complete and supported by valid excerpts whose stated premises entail it; no extra design rule is invented |
| 1 | No wrong/unsafe required decision, but a required decision is omitted, explicitly uncertain, materially conflicting, inferred without sufficient source support, or capped for quote fidelity |
| 0 | A wrong/unsafe required decision or no answer; a safe refusal to invent missing authority is 1, not 0 |

Grounded derivation is allowed: multiple quoted premises may entail a decision even when no single sentence spells it out.\
Do not require the candidate's wording, unasked subsidiary rules or details marked ancillary.\
When the predecessor leaves a required design choice unstated, an honest gap is textual fidelity but not complete implementation guidance; a lucky unsupported guess cannot score 2.\
This rule is version-neutral and cannot presuppose that the predecessor lacks support the auditor or reader can actually show.

Retain both cross-family scores and reasons; the project's conservative score is their minimum, then the quote cap.\
Any missing, malformed or nonmatching submitted excerpt caps a safe outcome at 1; it never raises a 0.\
Check every submitted excerpt against the assigned input, and separately check whether the excerpts actually support every decisive claim.

For each outcome, retain distinct diagnostic fields for wrong decision, missing required decision, missing semantic support and exact-quote/format defect.\
Judges are asked for `DECISION`, `COVERAGE`, `SUPPORT` and `HEDGE` tags plus a concrete reason; malformed diagnostic tags remain raw and unknown until a non-author records a reason-based classification.\
That classification does not change the judge score or substitute new votes, and diagnostic-tag formatting alone never earns a favorable retry.\
The quote check separately records absent, malformed and nonmatching excerpts, regardless of whether the cap changes the final score.

This separates causes without weakening the safe, complete, grounded score-2 requirement.\
Scores from this twenty-four-heading instrument must not be compared numerically with the previous twelve compound questions or used to regrade that run.

## Validity and full accounting

Account for all 144 reader/heading outcomes and 288 expected other-family vote slots, including absence and failure.\
Missing answers are deterministic zeroes with explicit not-cast vote slots, never invented judge votes.\
Every present answer needs two valid other-family votes under the pinned runner's contract; retain their original reasons and all attempts.

| Condition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Truncation, missing file/heading/answer or malformed quotation from a completed reader | Reader outcome, not an infrastructure retry; account for missing outcomes as zero |
| Runner-classified behavior failure | Retain and score reached material; unreached answers are zero; no retry |
| Infrastructure or isolation failure | Use the pinned runner's one permitted retry, retaining every attempt; unresolved failure leaves no gate verdict |
| Present answer lacks required valid votes or delivery/isolation provenance | No gate verdict; no substitution of author judgment for votes |
| Substantive key, question, delivery or scoring defect independently established after responses exist | Affected comparison has no gate verdict; preserve exploratory outcomes, correct prospectively and obtain new questions/readers |
| Valid low score, adverse observation or control regression | Apply the frozen rule; do not retry, reweight or edit toward a pass |

Inspect actual observations and all accepted vote reasons in both arms, not merely totals.\
A majority hedge is at least two of three readers in one arm/heading uncertain about a required decision; stating an intended scope exclusion is not a hedge.\
Investigate hedges against the actual assigned text before blaming the key.\
An unresolved material ambiguity in the candidate or comparison prevents PASS; a predecessor-only limitation does not become a candidate defect by attribution.

## Gate condition and judge

A non-author reviewer, not the packet author or a scored reader, judges the completed measurement against these conditions.\
The reviewer receives the frozen packet, all raw outcomes/votes/observations, exact-input quotation checks and complete conservative accounting; the runner's general-purpose tally is not this project's gate.

PASS requires all of the following:

1. Six measurement-usable reader outcomes under the validity rules, with complete accounting of every expected answer and vote slot.
2. For every candidate heading, at least two of three conservative scores equal 2, and no candidate score equals 0.
3. For each of the fifteen controls independently, the candidate's conservative sum is at least the predecessor's sum; other gains cannot compensate for a control regression.
4. At least one heading has a strictly higher candidate sum; an all-tie comparison retains the predecessor and does not establish improvement.
5. No unresolved candidate/comparison ambiguity or substantive measurement defect.

Failure of a condition in a valid measurement is FAIL; invalid required inputs leave no verdict.\
These thresholds are prospective decision criteria, not a significance test or a forecast that the clarification will pass.\
The earlier FAIL remains valid regardless of this result; ties or failure also do not reinstate earlier rejected designs.\
No result ratifies the public contract, verifies runtime behavior, certifies browser/scale/authentication guarantees or closes B53.
