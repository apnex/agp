SCORING, VALIDITY, AND GATE CONSISTENCY

[ASSESSMENT] Apart from KR-01, the declared scoring and adjudication rules are coherent for valid completed answer phases:

- The 0/1/2 distinction measures safe implementation completeness, not mere fidelity to a defective or incomplete document. Safe acknowledged absence scores at most 1, not 0; wrong required decisions or absent answers are 0 (preregistration lines 78-87).
- Several exact excerpts may jointly entail a decision. There is no quotation quota, preferred wording, one-quote-per-clause demand, or permission to import unquoted contracts (lines 74-87; experiment lines 25-26).
- The conservative result is the lower other-family score, then a downward-only exact-quotation cap. Whitespace-only normalization, every submitted excerpt, and separate semantic entailment are all explicit (lines 89-96).
- Exact quotation is not semantic support. The raw answer/input relation must be checked independently of the model judges; plausible quotations and successful parsing are not enough.
- Diagnostic tags retain wrong decision, omission, support and hedge distinctions. Missing/malformed tags stay raw and unknown until a non-author gives a reason-based classification; that process changes neither scores nor votes (lines 93-96).
- Six reader cells, 144 outcome slots and 288 other-family vote slots are predeclared. Missing answers in a valid phase are deterministic zeroes with not-cast slots; present judge-eligible answers need both votes. KR-01 is the required exception-boundary repair, not permission to reduce the denominator.
- Other-family exclusion is implemented by the pinned judge assignment (C/packages/core/src/judge-panel.ts:38-40). The panel uses fresh empty workspaces and tools-off invocations, and rejects tool-using judge replies (lines 199-213). Those are source observations, not proof of later execution.
- The pinned panel may request missing/invalid judge outputs up to three prompts, retaining replies (judge-panel.ts:199-221); that is distinct from the reader-cell one-rerun rule (run-conductor.ts:321-348). Diagnostic-tag repair alone must not trigger another judge request under the packet's own rule.
- Valid poor performance cannot be retried, reweighted or edited toward PASS. Invalid required measurement inputs yield no gate verdict; an independently established substantive post-response instrument defect requires a prospective new comparison, not retroactive rescoring (preregistration lines 109-116).
- A majority hedge is at least two readers in one arm/heading uncertain about an asked decision. Intended scope exclusions are not hedges. Both arms' answers, observations and vote reasons must be read, and the supplied text must be investigated before blaming the key (lines 118-121). A predecessor-only limit is not automatically a candidate ambiguity.
- The project gate is not the evaluator's mean-vote tally. It requires candidate per-heading completeness with no zero, control-by-control non-regression, a strict improvement somewhere, and no unresolved material candidate/comparison ambiguity or instrument defect (lines 127-141). Gains cannot offset a regressing control; an all-tie comparison cannot establish improvement.

The same profile serving as a reader in one new session and as a judge in another does not mean it judges its own family's outcome; the assignment code excludes that family.
It also does not make three families a calibrated independent statistical population.
