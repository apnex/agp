## 4. Separate decision correctness from document support

Declare two independently scored properties per probe using the same complete answer: decision correctness on `[0, 1]`, and grounded usability on `[0, 2]`.\
The public rendering must demonstrate that both properties receive the intended complete answer and their own rubric; there is no inferred multi-property support exemption.

| Axis | Proposed rule | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Decision correctness | `1` only when every asked decision is explicit, complete and correct; `0` for a wrong, missing or uncertain required decision. Ignore citations and whether a document was supplied | M2 separation and decision non-regression, on identical criteria in all three arms |
| Grounded usability | `2` for complete correct decisions supported by valid supplied-text premises; `1` for safe but incomplete, uncertain or insufficiently supported decisions; `0` for wrong/unsafe decisions or demonstrated absence | Candidate usability and old/new comparison; the empty arm's grounding values supply no favorable evidence |

Keep both original other-family votes and reasons; use their minimum for each axis.\
The exact-quote check can cap grounded usability at `1`, never increase a score, and never alter decision correctness.\
Grounded derivation from several valid premises is allowed; matching a quote is not proof that it entails the decision.\
Accurate proposal-status or intended-scope qualifications are not required-decision hedges.

Every judge receives the common proposal-status rule, exact question, complete required-decision key and axis-specific rubric through public fields.\
Key support must be independently checked against both full inputs before exposure, and completed-measurement review must inspect every raw answer, accepted reason and all three arms' observations against the assigned inputs.\
Malformed diagnostic tags do not replace the reason; an independently established substantive score/reason contradiction invalidates the comparison without rescoring, vote substitution or changing the document toward a pass.

### Prospective M2 separation rule

For each probe, compute the mean conservative decision score in each arm over all three assigned readers, including demonstrated behavioral zeroes.\
Propose a separation margin of one third: the absolute difference between either document arm's mean and the no-document mean must be at least one third.\
Use integer totals to avoid boundary rounding; either document arm may establish separation, in either direction, as M2 permits.

Favorable separation additionally requires all nine assigned answer units for that probe to be present, eligible and faithfully judged.\
Demonstrated absence stays zero in negative/regression accounting but cannot manufacture positive document-effect evidence through an empty control arm.\
An explicit inability to decide is a present answer scored under the decision rubric, not an absent record.

This is a preregistered descriptive threshold proposed for review, not a significance test or permission to choose a margin after results.\
No earlier record is credited as supplying this same-probe/no-document comparison.\
Controls must qualify by the same rule; citations, grounding scores and unequal missing-data denominators cannot create separation.

A nonseparating probe contributes no favorable evidence for the change.\
It still counts against the candidate if the candidate regresses, on either decision correctness or grounded usability.\
The packet must map qualifying probes to every required duty and public-migration concern; no uncovered concern can be declared assured because unrelated probes improved.

## 5. Validity and prospective gate

| Observation | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Complete, faithfully delivered low scores or a control regression | Preserve and apply the rule; do not retry, remove a family or shrink a denominator |
| Completed empty response or proved empty canonical section | Behavioral zero and explicit uncast slots; preserve the full outcome |
| Ambiguous/lost answer text, truncation that prevents faithful judging, missing required votes, unresolved isolation or missing attempt records | No assurance verdict; retain all surviving favorable and unfavorable evidence |
| Parent interruption with an incomplete invocation record | Invalidate the whole new comparison; no automatic resume, cell replacement, guessed outcome or splicing across runs |
| A valid completed comparison lacks separation/coverage or fails another pass condition | Assurance FAIL with the failed condition named; insufficient document-effect evidence is not a newly proved runtime defect |
| Independently established substantive question/key/delivery/scoring defect | No assurance verdict; correct prospectively, never regrade the old measurement |

An interruption is not a mechanism for erasing already observed problems.\
Retain the manifest, surviving records, declared missing identities and uncertainty; the absence of a record cannot prove that no billable invocation occurred.\
A rerun after invalidation needs a new explicit decision and recorded exposure disposition, not a silent restart until a favorable sample appears.

Proposed PASS requires all of the following after independent measurement-validity review:

1. Every assigned reader/answer/axis/vote slot is reconciled, with no unresolved delivery, isolation, extraction, record or substantive scoring defect.
2. Every candidate probe retains the prior usability floor: at least two of three conservative grounded scores equal `2`, and no candidate grounded score equals `0`.
3. Each control's candidate arm total is at least its predecessor total on both axes, with the same three-reader denominator; additionally, no nonseparating probe regresses on either axis. Gains on other probes cannot compensate.
4. Separating probes collectively cover all seven duties and public migration, and at least one separating probe strictly improves candidate grounded usability over the predecessor.
5. No unresolved material candidate/comparison ambiguity remains; majority uncertainty is checked against the supplied text before attributing it to readers.

These are proposed replacement conditions requiring review and later approval, not inherited thresholds or a current PASS.\
The 24-probe/two-axis/three-arm/three-reader design has 432 intended axis outcomes and 864 other-family vote slots; demonstrated absent answers retain uncast identities rather than invented votes.\
The evaluator's general tally does not itself decide this project gate, and no cross-run statistical comparison with the old instrument is claimed.

## 8. Mechanics rationale and consequence

### Mechanics

Qualify an existing public evaluator profile, compare fresh old/new/no-document readers under committed keys, and obtain an independent verdict from complete records.

### Rationale

Useful assurance needs both correct supported decisions and evidence that the document changes what readers decide.\
Hard provider caps and lossless parent-crash capture are no longer prerequisites for preparing that design.

### Consequence

Invalid or insufficient evidence cannot unblock B53; a successful document result still cannot claim a working AGP implementation.
