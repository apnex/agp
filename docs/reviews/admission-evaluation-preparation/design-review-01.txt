MECHANICS: SOURCE FINDINGS AND ADVERSARIAL ASSESSMENT

1. The repair addresses an observed defect without claiming its cause proved.
   I re-read the frozen preregistration's scoring/validity clauses, the actual
   A16 question, key, answer and Gemini raw reason in job
   `admission-clarity.answer.answers.g.15.json`, and candidate input status and
   recommendation text. The accepted penalty used accurate proposal status as
   its sole stated hedge despite correct/complete/supported required decisions.
   The design's lines 26-32 preserve completeness and support requirements while
   distinguishing that status caveat from actual required-decision uncertainty.
   They neither mandate score 2 for mentioning proposal status nor excuse an
   answer that withholds a required decision. This is a prospective correction;
   it does not regrade the old answer or prove why that judge made its error.

2. One delivered policy is a real seam, not an alternate judge implementation.
   At context-lab candidate `0c0ab1f0248ef0cbb21885d7d6af76586d8a1b68`,
   `packages/core/src/judge-panel.ts` has the same Git blob as at historical
   `6e6d0a92965a15ec473c661887b2b9c5037fedcf`:
   306d7da951a88cefcdcfe0bc923ee4bbddab3070.
   Its lines 95-110 deliver custom judge instructions, question, key, rubric
   and answer; the generic fallback is replaced, not appended. Lines 135-152
   accept integer score range/association and stringify the reason, without
   adjudicating its meaning. Lines 175-224 assign other-family judges, choose
   the adapter's delivery mode, invoke tools-off, and retain job replies.
   `index.ts` exports `judgeAll`. Thus the proposed capture injection can check
   the real public rendering/assignment/parser seam without copying it.
   It cannot show an actual runtime delivered the prompt or obeyed isolation.

3. The checker must detect contradiction, not become an automatic scorer.
   Design lines 40-53 expressly preserve the original score/reason and require
   independent semantic review of full-credit votes as well as penalties.
   Falsifiable failure hypotheses are: a correct-looking tag record conceals
   a wrong decision; an accurate caveat is falsely assigned a required ID; a
   score-1 record has no actual deficiency; or a quote cap creates an apparent
   contradiction when applied before this checker. G1-G3 below hold these
   boundaries. Mechanical cleanliness supplies no entitlement to full credit.

4. Absence needs two distinct test surfaces.
   The design includes an absent-answer diagnostic fixture, but the selected
   proposal preserves deterministic zero outcomes and explicit not-cast slots.
   Testing syntax for `DECISION=missing`, `REQUIRED=all`, `ISSUE=no-answer`
   is legitimate as a checker unit test; inserting such a vote into an actual
   absent reader outcome would contradict the frozen accounting rule.
   G3 is mandatory. No denominator, eligible population or threshold changes.

5. The public cap is configuration, not an observed effective budget.
   Candidate `adapters.ts:179-191` accepts positive integral `outputTokenMax`;
   lines 216-225 put it into the explicit OpenCode sandbox environment.
   Lines 167-177 disclose a configured route; this is not an SDK/wire/backend
   trace. Codex lines 297-309 accept only `reasoningEffort`, not the cap.
   These source facts support static seam preparation only. I did not re-probe
   OpenCode, infer a cause of the historical Gemini stops, or select a token
   value. The design explicitly distinguishes these levels at lines 64-72.

6. The whole candidate upgrade has a live-adoption blocker.
   Independently confirmed in `isolation-prober.ts:72-88`: a tool-using first
   probe can be retried, the local outcome is replaced, and returned answers
   describe only the final attempt. Final-only returned tool counts therefore
   cannot prove that every attempt was tools-off. This is source-derived, not
   a reproduced runtime failure or a claim that it happened in the old run.
   The candidate diff spans 30 files; unchanged judge-panel code does not
   qualify its conductor/probe/accounting changes. The separate compatibility
   record preserves this blocker. The earlier cap commit
   `9a9b5e96c484b37d3f3fdbf34c0e7b726747a0a1` shares the old probe blob
   `bdb5c0b7b4d7be9fb6b6c6c5b0dfd9a69e57d9fb`, which is an alternative

AXIOM MAPPING

The bounded system holds durable evaluation records, coordinates independent
authors/reviewers and future readers/judges, and puts model judgment on the
eventual critical path. Stateful, multi-agent and LLM-in-the-loop commitments
therefore apply. The deterministic specification seam is considered below;
this report does not claim the broader system is a reconciled declarative one.

- A1, load-bearing: raw prompts/replies/votes and exact identities survive the
  checker. Final-only probe retention is explicitly blocked from live adoption.
- A2, supporting: one policy generates both delivered instruction surfaces,
  checked byte-for-byte. This reduces specification drift; it is not proof of
  a fully isomorphic/reconciling evaluation system.
- A3, load-bearing: AGP owns scoring semantics; context-lab owns dispatch and
  runtime mechanisms. Public injection is allowed; copied parser/conductor or
  private monkey-patching to bypass the upstream gap is not.
- A4, load-bearing: preserve historical raw evidence and verdicts verbatim;
  preparation captures and future request observations remain distinct records.
- A5, load-bearing: explicit common proposal status and required decisions reach
  every judge profile. Rendered parity is not assumed to establish live parity.
- A6, supporting: generate the shared policy instead of manually transcribing
  divergent instructions; independent review does not transfer author authority.
- A7, load-bearing: diagnostic findings stop promotion with actionable issues;
  failed/absent outcomes remain visible, never silently replaced by successes.
- A8, load-bearing: design/M7 precede code; exact-artifact readiness follows;
  live observation/calibration and fresh assurance have separate later gates.
- A9, not materially implicated as deployment proof at this gate: no production
  or live runtime is promoted. Static evidence must not acquire that claim.
- A10, supporting: the observed evaluator friction becomes an explicit repair
  and compatibility gap, without inventing an AGP mechanism defect.
- A11, load-bearing: syntax, ID membership, contradiction detection and complete
  accounting belong in code; semantic entailment remains independent judgment.
- A12, supporting: explicit diagnostic structure and future budget observations
  make context cost inspectable. Savings, sufficiency and live caps are unmeasured;
  historical complete inputs cannot be trimmed merely to improve completion.
- A13, load-bearing: preparation consumes no model budget and preselects neither
  token values nor failure-policy changes. Live authority remains the director's.
- A14, load-bearing: prior lessons become prospective invariants and ordinary
  static checks; they do not become reused assurance cases or forgotten blockers.

LAYERS AND TENSIONS

- Intent/authority: the selected move prepares an instrument, not an AGP redesign
  or live launch. Economy of director attention does not authorize missing choices.
- State/knowledge: immutable old records coexist with separately labelled
  development captures. Compact findings reference full evidence rather than
  substituting for it; no session-memory-only prerequisite is accepted.
- Specification/composition: generated policy, pure checker and public panel
  have separate duties. Shared generation prevents drift; independent literal
  semantic expectations must prevent a shared incorrect rule from passing itself.
- Perception/context: common proposal status is given uniformly, without revealing
  which document is preferred. It cannot replace decisive quoted support.
- Operations/integrity: synthetic jobs exercise the declared public seam only.
  Whole-runtime retry/retention drift cannot inherit that seal. Resilience does
  not permit favorable retries that erase failed attempts from the measurement.
- Learning: the upstream attempt-retention gap is durable and must be revisited
  before live selection. It does not justify expanding this preparation into a
  harness fork, unapproved diagnostic, or iterative AGP redesign.

DELTAS AND IMPLEMENTATION GUARDRAILS

No blocking design rewrite or director override is required for this static
preparation. The following are conditions of this PASS, not optional polish:

G1. Run the pure diagnostic checker on original accepted judge votes, before
    conservative aggregation and exact-quote caps. Retain raw score/reason and
    every finding. Never coerce, erase, rescore, substitute or favorably retry a
    vote; a flag is neither a fabricated missing vote nor a semantic conviction.
G2. Keep score 2 contingent on every numbered required decision being correct,
    complete, settled and sufficiently grounded. Valid IDs/tags alone prove none
    of that. Preserve negative cases for real uncertainty, omissions, unsupported
    decisions and wrong decisions, including answers that invoke proposal status
    while actually declining an asked decision. Test the policy's expected meaning,
    not only a renderer compared with another output of the same renderer.
G3. Keep absent-answer diagnostic fixtures checker-only. Actual absent/ended
    outcomes remain deterministic zeroes plus explicit not-cast slots, with no
    invented judge votes. Keep all six readers, other-family assignments, complete
    denominators, conservative aggregation and quote caps unchanged.
G4. The public-panel integration must inject local capture-only adapters with
    no provider-capable fallback, use the real supported delivery modes, require
    tools off, retain complete units/jobs/prompts/replies/votes and verify exact
    assignments. Well-formed fixtures require no retries. All records are synthetic
    development evidence; neither a reader probe nor a conductor run is in scope.
G5. No execution/registration path, real adapter invocation, model experiment,
    shared-config change or sibling mutation. Source/route/refusal checks must use
    only sanitized explicit configuration. Route disclosure is not a general-purpose
    secret scrubber, request capture or backend attestation.
G6. No live cap selected by a synthetic test value; no Codex cap setting. Preserve
    separately configured override, client-emitted request and backend-received/
    enforced value, allowing unknowns. Retain visible output, reasoning use, finish
    reason, file result and elapsed time independently when later authorized.

Durable follow-ups, not implementation permission:

- PREP-L1, runtime adoption: on any proposed live pin selection, re-triage the
  probe retry/all-attempt-recording gap and the changed public consumers. Qualify
  a compatible upstream revision or a reviewed upstream policy/recording seam;
  do not select the earlier commit or waive the gap by implication.
- PREP-L2, live diagnostics/calibration: only on a separate director decision,
  freeze finite call/cost ceilings, exact caps, sanitized observation method,
  unrelated cases/expected outcomes and stop rules. Representative volume and
  file-writing behavior remain unmeasured. No endless calibration until success.
- PREP-L3, fresh assurance: only after calibration qualification and separate
  authority, independently audit new questions and fresh sessions for both
  unchanged inputs. Preserve the completed-measurement independent semantic
  review and post-response substantive-defect invalidation rule.
