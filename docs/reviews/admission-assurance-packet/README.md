## Scoring and complete gate

Each answer has decision correctness `D` in `[0,1]` and grounded usability `G` in `[0,2]`, each judged by both other families.\
Keep every accepted vote/reason and take the minimum independently on each axis.\
Exact-quote failure caps `G` at one without increasing zero or altering `D`; matching a quotation never proves entailment.\
Accurate proposal-status qualifications are not decision uncertainty.

For each probe, use fixed three-reader totals in each arm.\
Separation requires an absolute old-versus-none or new-versus-none decision-total difference of at least one, with all nine answers present, eligible and faithfully judged.\
Absence stays zero adversely but cannot supply favorable separation.\
This is a descriptive one-third margin, not statistical significance or family-wise non-regression.

After independent measurement-validity review, PASS requires:

1. Every assigned slot reconciled without unresolved delivery, isolation, extraction, record or substantive scoring defects.
2. Every candidate probe has at least two grounded twos and no grounded zero.
3. Every probe has nonnegative candidate-minus-old totals on each axis separately; no compensation by another probe, axis or classification.
4. Separating probes cover authority, lifetime, FIFO, route, cleanup, supervisor, operations and public migration, with at least one separating strict grounded improvement.
5. No material unresolved candidate/comparison ambiguity; independently inspect complete observations and majority uncertainty against the assigned text.

There are nine readers, 216 answer units, 432 axis outcomes and 864 other-family vote slots.\
A proved empty answer retains two zero outcomes and four explicit uncast slots; a whole empty reader retains forty-eight zero outcomes and ninety-six uncast slots.\
Missing, truncated, unassignable or unfaithful evidence gives NO VERDICT, not a fabricated absence or smaller denominator.\
Retain all computable adverse differences even in an invalid comparison.

The arithmetic helper consumes a reconciliation ledger, not raw model output.\
Each row identifies probe/arm/member, the public cell/selected-unit locator, faithful delivery/eligibility, quote-check result and both original other-family votes with attempt, reason and job locator.\
Absent rows need a proved `whole-response` or `empty-section` classification and explicit null vote/attempt slots.\
Its independent-review fields must refer to an actual complete non-author review of public records, not author-populated assertions; the helper cannot certify the truth of those attestations.

No runtime-record importer or private answer reconstruction is supplied.\
Only context-lab's public extractor may produce judged answers; the shape check rejects but does not repair.\
Before any real gate use, a non-author must verify raw-to-bounded-to-selected-to-masked-to-judged fidelity, original vote attribution, complete observations and every accepted semantic reason.\
Contradictory accepted scores/reasons or a grounded two with an incompatible decision score invalidate evidence, not invite regrading.
