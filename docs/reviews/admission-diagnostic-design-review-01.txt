BLOCKING FINDING AND REQUIRED DELTAS

F1 - Resource/authority ambiguity, not a numerical miscount.
Proposal:36-43 allocates 36 runtime invocations and 120 provider requests to
stages 1-3 and recommends USD 20 "total maximum attributable spend".
Proposal:102,116,127,131 also requires independent semantic assessment of all
42 calibration reasons and six 24-question reader outputs, with judgment
between stages. It never names that review path or states whether ordinary
author/reviewer session work is outside the dollar claim. Nor does it account
for additional driven/model-based reviews if those are used.

Two implementers could therefore follow the text differently: one uses already
authorized non-author review work, while another adds a paid scoring/review
round outside the enumerated calls yet still reports the headline total.
The latter would introduce hidden calls, cost and possibly altered independence.
I did not observe such calls; this is a design ambiguity with a concrete
downstream consumer, not a measured overrun. The author's later explanation
of intent does not amend the frozen target.

D1 - Before implementation, name the semantic-review workflow and its authority.
Explicitly distinguish ordinary authorized author/reviewer work from additional
driven/model/provider review invocations. If ordinary session work is outside
the proposed monetary envelope, name the envelope accordingly and say that the
excluded work is not free or included. Prohibit relabeling an extra experiment,
scorer or model invocation as ordinary review. Any additional driven review
must have prospectively selected call/request/spend accounting; recompute the
ceilings if it is included, or obtain explicit separate authority. Record which
review is required after each stage and how its completion controls progression.
This review does not select the future reviewer, resources or monetary allowance.

D2 - Order Stage 0 explicitly: capability/trace/containment feasibility FIRST,
before producing the two long documents, authoring a driver or investing in the
full packet. Inventory only existing supported mechanisms and already accepted
evidence. An unavailable/unknown prerequisite must produce a terminal zero-call
gap record, with owner and revival trigger. Preparing a request for later
approval is permitted by the proposal; sending it or implementing generic
budget/trace infrastructure in AGP is not. This makes the proposal's existing
stop rule operational and keeps a bounded diagnostic from becoming an evaluator
programme by sunk-cost pressure.

A revised proposal addressing D1/D2 needs a separate exact-artifact design
recheck. No implementation started, no override was observed, and this FAIL
does not overturn the earlier static qualification or the evaluation-repair
purpose.

  The provider/account might have a supported facility elsewhere; that was
  neither established nor disproved. The design correctly refuses to assume
  it exists. Missing support is a zero-call readiness stop, not permission

- Source-derived retention/stop risk: judgeAll stores initial job prompt and

  exceptions/deadline loss. Missing evidence must remain missing, never an

SCOPED M7 MAPPING, LAYERS AND TENSIONS

The changed procedure holds durable records, coordinates independent author/
reviewer roles and places model judgment on the critical path. Stateful,
multi-agent and LLM-in-the-loop commitments bind. It composes with unchanged
context-lab; it does not claim responsibility for all of that component's internals.
This is not a fully reconciled declarative system, so A2 is supporting direction.

A1  load-bearing: durable reservations, stop latch and complete identity maps;
    no invisible consumption or unrecoverable in-process-only audit claims.
A2  supporting: selected limits/roles become guarded configuration; old profile
    cannot silently drift into a live one.
A3  load-bearing: existing public seams; missing generic budget/trace facilities
    remain owner-side gaps. D2 prevents consumer-local evaluator accretion.
A4  load-bearing: preserve prior failed history and every actual attempt;
    no final-only record is reported as complete request evidence.
A5  load-bearing: reviewers receive full source/key/answer/reason relations;
    client declaration, emitted request and backend observation remain distinct.
A6  supporting: reuse policy and public components; explicit reviewer handoff
    avoids repeated experiments or a human relaying missing records.
A7  load-bearing: typed fail-closed stops and durable unknown reservations;
    stopping a diagnostic is not permission to heal it by favorable retry.
A8  load-bearing: design, exact readiness, director execution choice and
    completed judgment are separate gates. This material design gap stays FAIL.
A9  not materially implicated as deployment qualification: no production change
    or resilience proof is offered; diagnostic success cannot imply either.
A10 supporting: unavailable capabilities become precise separately selected
    owner-side work, not a hidden workaround.
A11 load-bearing: deterministic counting/quote/identity checks; genuinely
    cognitive semantic judgment stays independent, with D1's explicit resources.
A12 load-bearing: role-specific caps and meaningful distinct workload; no
    byte-padding substitute or claim that a configured cap is backend behavior.
A13 load-bearing, unresolved: proposal preparation is not spending authority;
    D1 prevents a nominal total ceiling from hiding extra review work.
A14 load-bearing: bank exact failure/gap records with revival conditions;
    bounded learning must not turn into an open-ended evaluator project.

Layers: intent selects preparation only; configuration binds role/profile and
resource units; composition uses public runtime/panel/episode seams; state/audit
retains reservations and attempts; perception separates declarations from actual
observations; integrity gates semantic judgment before progression; learning
routes missing prerequisites to separately approved work.

Tensions and resolutions:
- Independent semantic quality versus finite resources: required judgment cannot
  be omitted or declared free. D1 must make the review and budget boundary explicit.
- Useful representative workload versus speculative preparation: D2 performs
  capability checks first; no long-fixture/driver investment on an unknown path.
- Strict stops versus built-in retries: preserve source-owned missing-vote
  retries only while the global admission latch remains open.
- Learning versus historical integrity: one bounded diagnostic may fail or stop;
  it does not tune against fresh assurance, regrade history or change AGP.
- Source knowledge versus live claims: exact public fit is necessary, not proof
  that provider budgets, transport, isolation or reader completion work.
