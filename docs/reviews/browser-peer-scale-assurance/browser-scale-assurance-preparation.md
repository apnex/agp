## 3. Reader population and judging

Proposed population: three fresh readers per reading, one from each of three distinct model families, with the same profiles and settings on both sides.\
That is six reader cells, 48 question outcomes and 96 expected other-family vote slots, including explicit missing outcomes and not-cast slots.\
This is a small descriptive comparison, not within-family replication or a statistical estimate of general model performance.

The author, key auditor and sessions carrying earlier AGP context cannot serve as scored readers.\
Readers receive only their assigned text, common questions and instructions; no repository, alternate reading, key, role map or conversation history.\
Each eligible answer requires both other-family judgments under the [context-lab contract](../../../../mission-kit/components/C2-context-lab.md); the final reviewer is neither author nor scored reader.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Complete, correct and grounded interpretation of the assigned text, including a justified absence or qualified inference |
| 1 | Safe but incomplete, uncertain or insufficiently grounded |
| 0 | Materially wrong decision, invented authority or guarantee, or absent substantive answer from an eligible completed reader |

Code checks quotation membership in the assigned text using whitespace normalization only; judges decide entailment and whether an absence or inference is justified.\
A positive claim without valid support caps an otherwise safe answer at 1 and never raises a zero.\
Use the lower of the two valid other-family scores and that cap; retain the original votes, reasons, excerpts and separate diagnoses.

## 4. Proposed comparison rule

Every expected cell, question and vote slot must be reconciled before scoring.\
Unestablished delivery, isolation, invocation completion or answer/judge eligibility produces `NO VERDICT`, not a zero or a favorable missing-data rule.\
An eligible completed reader's missing answer scores zero with an explicit reason; a present answer's quote or formatting defect is not automatically absence.

A proposed documentation-comprehension PASS requires all five conditions:

1. Complete eligible accounting, including both required judgments for every present eligible answer.
2. Every candidate question has at least two conservative scores of 2 and no zero.
3. No control's candidate sum is below its baseline sum; gains elsewhere cannot offset a control regression.
4. At least one question has a higher candidate sum; an all-tie result retains the baseline on measured comprehension and demonstrates no improvement.
5. All raw observations and reasons have been inspected, with no unresolved material ambiguity or instrument defect.

A valid comparison missing a condition is FAIL under this proposed rule; a substantive key, projection, extraction or scoring defect is `NO VERDICT`.\
Two of three readers hedging on the same decision triggers an ambiguity review before blaming the key; a deliberately unselected quantity is not a hedge.\
No selective rerun, post-answer key change or reweighting is permitted.

Different faithful policy answers are not a score gain.\
A tie or failure to show clearer comprehension does not refute the director's deployment need, and neither PASS nor a target supplies ratification or capacity evidence.\
The strict-improvement rule is proposed for pre-run audit, not permission to narrow an honest baseline answer until the candidate wins.

## 5. Execution prerequisites

Context-lab owns execution, isolation, extraction, judging and replay records; AGP owns this experiment and its conclusion.\
The projection and packet checks launch no agents and implement no scoring engine.\
This directory is a source packet, not a validated executable context-lab pack.

| Boundary | Required before scored execution |
|---|---|
| Input delivery | Same instructions/questions and exactly one reading; prove assigned delivery and isolation rather than relying on a warning not to look |
| Reader payload | At most 64 KiB UTF-8 for reading, instructions and questions; separately account for wrappers and the selected model's context limit without silent input truncation |
| Response | Same 24 KiB UTF-8 bound for every reader; validate extraction, completion and overflow treatment before use; a scored-text bound is not a capture-memory or spending bound |
| Judge delivery | Exact common rules plus the applicable reading-specific key and assigned-text support; no role/order metadata or alternate reading; validate a public binding before selecting serialization or batching |
| Complete accounting | Record every planned cell, answer and vote slot, every dispatch and attempt, effective retry policy, eligibility and actual prompts; reconcile interrupted or absent records without inventing completion |
| Resources | Pin component/runtime/model profiles, request/time/spending bounds and retention; obtain separate execution authority |
