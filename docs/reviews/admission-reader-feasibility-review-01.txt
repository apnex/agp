### Findings

1. **The old numerical envelope is not incorrectly made mandatory.**  
   [Feasibility line 44](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/admission-reader-feasibility.md:44) explicitly says the 36-invocation, 120-request and USD 20 envelope “does not automatically transfer.” [Approval-route lines 83–84](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/admission-lifetime-approval-route.md:83) distinguish session ceilings from request/currency ceilings and require agreement on a different risk boundary. M2 requires fresh, blinded readers and precommitted, independently audited probes; it does not prescribe those numerical caps. The stop is therefore supported as a finding under these proposed guarantees, not a universal prerequisite for every conceivable M2 route.

2. **Neither ordinary shared agents nor tools-off review establishes the missing conjunction of guarantees.**  
   The active collaboration declaration preserves shared filesystem access; omitting conversation does not remove that access. [M2 lines 51–56](/home/apnex/taceng/mission-kit/methods/M2-test-drive-docs-by-execution.md:51) require readers holding only the document, without repository or session history. The pinned operations guide, lines 527–531, declares tools-off review and structured findings, but not complete billable-request accounting or an experimental comparison protocol. Tools-off remains a useful supported review capability; it is not demonstrated to satisfy all proposed reader-route requirements.

3. **Resource and interruption gaps are evidenced, not invented.**  
   The [corrected owner response, lines 52–55 and 64–67](/home/apnex/taceng/context-lab/incoming/agp-admission-diagnostic-correction-response.md:52), distinguishes unsupported context-lab controls from unknown account controls and explicitly identifies loss of buffered in-flight events after parent termination. Its lines 74–86 label possible remedies as unimplemented owner-side work. The [compatibility response](/home/apnex/taceng/context-lab/incoming/agp-assurance-compatibility-correction-response.md:32) retains that exclusion; line 54 excludes generation-memory and billing bounds, and line 72 separates probe/judge retries from cell retry limits. Fewer assignments, response truncation or refusal to resume cannot substitute for those guarantees.

4. **The new addendum improves evidence but does not close these gaps.**  
   [Addendum lines 27–39](/home/apnex/taceng/context-lab/incoming/agp-context-lab-addendum-2026-10-20.md:27) correct assigned-reading/answer separation; lines 48–49 add delivery, probe-attempt, cost and dissent reporting. The pinned operations guide, line 431, defines `costs` as statistics over counted cells’ scored attempts—not a complete provider-request or monetary ledger. The addendum also leaves B21 open and expressly claims no AGP isolation result or scored run at line 59. These improvements warrant later compatibility triage, not automatic B53 qualification.

5. **The proposed disposition preserves authority and the existing component boundary.**  
   [Feasibility lines 65–72](/home/apnex/taceng/agp/docs/reviews/admission-reader-feasibility.md:65) distinguish waiting for evidence, prospectively changing risk boundaries, an explicit assurance exception, and selecting other work. This is consistent with [E7’s distinction between evidence, no verdict and override](/home/apnex/taceng/mission-kit/entities/E7-gate.md:43). The reviewed BOARD changes retain those distinctions. Stopping before another harness also respects [C2’s public-contract fit rule and upstream adjustment policy](/home/apnex/taceng/mission-kit/components/C2-context-lab.md:59).
